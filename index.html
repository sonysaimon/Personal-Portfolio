About

I started out building small tools for friends and never really stopped. These days I work across the stack, from designing an interface in Figma to shipping the backend that powers it. I like clear code, honest interfaces, and shipping things people can use today.

When I'm not at a keyboard I'm usually outside, reading, or trying to cook something I saw once and can't quite remember.
