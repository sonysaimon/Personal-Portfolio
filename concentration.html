Concentration

What I'm focused on right now. One main thing, a few supporting things, and a list of what I've set aside so the main thing can happen.

Updated August 20, 2026

Finishing the Main Street signage survey

Forty-one storefronts photographed, twenty-six written up. The goal for this season is to complete the remaining entries, map them, and publish the project page with a short essay on what the survey shows about language and ownership along one street.

Supporting work

  1. 01

    Weekly walks, same route

    Main Street from Terminal to 33rd, every Sunday, same time. Consistency makes change visible.

  2. 02

    Interviews with three signwriters

    Two are scheduled. The third has painted signs in the neighbourhood for thirty years and has not returned my call yet.

  3. 03

    Reading on linguistic landscape

    Working through the literature on signage and language policy so the essay stands on more than my own observations.
