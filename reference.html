Reference

Books, archives, and sources the project leans on, with a line on why each one matters here.

Visual culture and the city

  • Ways of SeeingJohn Berger

    The starting point for thinking about how images carry power. Short, still sharp.

  • The Image of the CityKevin Lynch

    How people build mental maps of where they live. Useful for thinking about landmarks like the gate.

  • Learning from Las VegasVenturi, Scott Brown, and Izenour

    Signs as architecture. The neon project owes this book a great deal.

Cultural geography and diaspora

  • Cultural Geography: A Critical IntroductionDon Mitchell

    The clearest account I've found of culture as something made in place, not just found there.

  • Cartographies of DiasporaAvtar Brah

    On diaspora space and who gets to belong. Underpins the Chinatown entries.

  • Saltwater CityPaul Yee

    A history of Chinese Canadians in Vancouver. Essential background for the archive.

Method

  • Doing Visual EthnographySarah Pink

    How to make photographs part of research rather than illustration for it.

  • Wanderlust: A History of WalkingRebecca Solnit

    Not a method book, but the reason walking is the method.

  • Vancouver NeonCity of Vancouver Archives

    Digitised photographs of signage from the 1950s onward. Where the neon project started.
