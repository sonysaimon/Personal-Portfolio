Chinatown Gate

 Next entry
Location
Keefer Street, Vancouver
Date
February 20, 2026
Observation
The gate marks the entrance to Chinatown with traditional architectural elements, vibrant colours, and Chinese characters. Traffic passes beneath it without slowing; most people photographing it are visitors.
Cultural / historical context
Built to mark the neighbourhood's long Chinese Canadian history, the gate stands where the community's presence has been contested, celebrated, and at times erased.
Personal interpretation
Every time I walk through this gate I feel like I'm stepping into a place where history and everyday life overlap. It is a threshold in both directions.
Academic connection
This observation connects to my interest in cultural geography, diaspora studies, and the visual politics of space. See Reference for the reading behind it.
